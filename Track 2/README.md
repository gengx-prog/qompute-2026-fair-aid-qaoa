The original `Track2_challenge_2_tutorial.ipynb` is the event's Max-Cut tutorial. This project applies QAOA to selecting aid projects under a limited budget. It adds a constraint penalty, a policy priority weight, X/XY ansatz comparisons, classical baselines, score perturbations, and a classical analysis of minimum project quotas. All inputs are synthetic teaching scores; the project does not claim quantum advantage or real-world allocation validity.
